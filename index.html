Frequently Asked

Questions Nigerian SMEs
ask before they switch to managed IT.

Quick answers to what most owners and operators want to know before booking an IT audit.

What does Vodatrox do?

Vodatrox is a Nigerian managed IT services provider. We act as an outsourced IT department for growing SMEs, handling support, cybersecurity, cloud, networks, custom software and strategic IT planning for a predictable monthly fee.

How is outsourced managed IT cheaper than hiring an in-house IT team?

A real internal IT team needs three to five specialised hires (IT manager, system administrator, security engineer, helpdesk) plus pension, benefits, equipment and training. Most SMEs cannot sustain that cost. Vodatrox delivers the same multi-disciplinary capability for less than the loaded cost of a single mid-level IT hire.

How fast does Vodatrox respond to critical IT issues?

We respond to critical issues in under 15 minutes and resolve standard support requests same-day. These targets are written into how we work and reviewed quarterly with every client.

What is included in Vodatrox's managed IT service?

Help desk and onsite support, cybersecurity and endpoint protection, cloud and infrastructure management, network and internet uptime, backup and disaster recovery, vendor management, quarterly strategic IT planning and ongoing technology roadmap reviews.

Does Vodatrox also build custom software?

Yes. We build ERP systems, internal portals, CRMs, automation tools and integrations for clients whose off-the-shelf software will not fit. Custom software is also part of our managed IT engagements when systems need building or extending over time.

What industries does Vodatrox serve?

We work with growing SMEs across healthcare and clinics, schools and education, financial services, law and professional services, logistics and supply chain, retail and e-commerce, hospitality and real estate.

Where is Vodatrox located?

Vodatrox is headquartered at 24b Bashorun Okunsanya Street, Lekki Phase 1, Lagos, Nigeria. We serve clients across Nigeria with remote support and same-day onsite response in major business centres.

How do I get started with Vodatrox?

Every engagement begins with a free 30-minute IT audit. We assess your current setup, identify gaps and tell you what is costing you money, with no contract required. From there we propose a managed IT scope, response targets and monthly fee.
